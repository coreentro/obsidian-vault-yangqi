# Avatar Design: Future Explorer

## Intent

Create an immediately beautiful, unmistakably high-technology avatar whose human gaze still conveys curiosity and long-horizon exploration.

## Final Design

- Square close portrait of one gender-ambiguous future explorer.
- Sleek original black-titanium research helmet with a strong, memorable silhouette.
- Narrow transparent visor reveals only two calm, focused human eyes.
- Refined cyan light channels with one restrained warm-amber accent.
- Dark clean background with subtle depth, not a busy science-fiction scene.
- Premium cinematic 3D realism with precise materials and lighting.

## Constraints

- Beautiful and immediately legible at 64 × 64 pixels and under circular cropping.
- Human eyes remain the focal point; helmet remains the dominant silhouette.
- No text, logo, brand resemblance, robot skull, exposed brain, circuit-face pattern, galaxy skin, weapons, aggression, horror, clutter, or cheap neon overload.

## Acceptance Criteria

- First impression: sophisticated, powerful technology avatar.
- Second impression: composed human intelligence looking beyond the present.
- Correct eye anatomy, believable visor reflections, coherent helmet construction.
