Rational construction of S-scheme CdS quantum dots/In2O3 hollow 
nanotubes heterojunction for enhanced photocatalytic H2 evolution 
Yong-Hui Wu, Yu-Qing Yan, Yi-Xiang Deng, Wei-Ya Huang, Kai Yang, Kang-Qiang Lu * 
Jiangxi Provincial Key Laboratory of Functional Crystalline Materials Chemistry, School of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering, Jiangxi University of 
Science and Technology, Ganzhou 341000, Jiangxi, China 
A R T I C L E  I N F O A B S T R A C T
Article history: 
Received 19 October 2024 
Accepted 28 November 2024 
Available online 20 March 2025 
The rapid recombination of photogenerated carriers poses a significant limitation on the use of CdS 
quantum dots (QDs) in photocatalysis. Herein, the construction of a novel S-scheme heterojunction 
between cubic-phase CdS QDs and hollow nanotube In2O3 is successfully achieved using an electro-
static self-assembly method. Under visible light irradiation, all CdS-In₂O₃ composites exhibit higher 
hydrogen evolution efficiency compared to pure CdS QDs. Notably, the photocatalytic H2 evolution 
rate of the optimal CdS-7%In2O3 composite is determined to be 2258.59 μmol g−1 h−1, approximately
12.3 times higher than that of pure CdS. The cyclic test indicates that the CdS- In₂O₃ composite 
maintains considerable activity even after 5 cycles, indicating its excellent stability. In situ X- ray 
photoelectron spectroscopy and density functional theory calculations confirm that carrier migra-
tion in CdS-In2O3 composites adheres to a typical S-scheme heterojunction mechanism. Additionally,
a series of characterizations demonstrate that the formation of S-scheme heterojunctions between 
In2O3 and CdS inhibits charge recombination and accelerates the separation and migration of pho-
togenerated carriers in the CdS QDs, thus achieving enhanced photocatalytic per formance. This 
work elucidates the pivotal role of S-scheme heterojunctions in photocatalytic H2 production and 
offers novel insights into the construction of effective composite photocatalysts.

1. Introduction 
The energy crisis and environmental pollution represent 
two of the most significant challenges facing human society 
today, seriously affecting its progress [1–4]. Consequently, the 
development and utilization of clean energy and the realization 
of green development are of paramount importance for social 
development. Recently, photocatalytic technology has gained 
attention as an effective solution to challenging environmental 
issues and energy demands owing to its efficient conversion of 
solar energy into chemical energy [5–7]. Among these, photo-
catalytic hydrogen production is arguably one of the most sig-
nificant photocatalytic technologies, employing a green and 
non-polluting approach [8–11]. However, to achieve high effi-
ciency in photocatalytic hydrogen production, enhancing the 
separation of electron-hole pairs and the utilization efficiency 
of charge carriers is essential [12–14]. Various strategies and 
catalytic systems have been developed for this purpose. Among

CdS is a popular photocatalytic hydrogen production mate-
rial owing to its narrow bandgap and easily adjustable surface 
structure [18,19]. In particular, CdS has a more negative con-
duction band (CB) potential than that of H
2/H2O, which is con-
ducive to H 2 production [20]. Furthermore, the quantum size 
effect of CdS quantum dots (QDs) enhances their photocatalytic 
performance, and the tunability of their band gap enables them 
to absorb a broader range of visible light [21]. Nevertheless, 
CdS exhibits severe photogenerated carrier recombination and 
is susceptible to hole oxidation decomposition, which signifi-
cantly limits its application in photocatalysis [22–24]. To ad-
dress these issues, several effective strategies have been pro-
posed, including morphological regulation, element doping, and 
heterojunction construction [25–28]. Among these strategies, 
the construction of an S-scheme heterojunction using another 
suitable semiconductor is promising. The construction of an 
S-scheme heterojunction enables oxidation and reduction reac-
tions to occur at separate sites, thereby promoting the spatial 
separation of photogenerated charges [29–33]. For instance, 
Zhang et al. [34] fabricated an S-scheme heterojunction of 
ZnIn
2S4/SnS2 QDs using a self-assembly approach and achieved 
enhanced photocatalytic H2 production performance. Sun et al. 
[35] deposited CdZnS QDs on the surface of g-C 3N4 using an 
electrostatic self-assembly method, and the composite demon-
strated efficient separation of photogenerated carriers. These 
examples illustrate that constructing S-scheme heterojunctions 
is an efficient approach for enhancing the photocatalytic activi-
ty and stability of QDs. In
2O3 has emerged as a subject of in-
tense research interest due to its low toxicity, suitable bandgap 
energy, and extensive visible light absorption [36,37]. Morpho-
logical control is a common method used to enhance the pho-
tocatalytic performance of semiconductors. For example, the 
hollow nanotube structure of In
2O3 has a high specific surface 
area, providing a large number of reaction sites. Furthermore, 
light reflection caused by the hollow structure can enhance the 
absorption and utilization efficiency of solar light. However, 
studies on S-scheme CdS-In2O3 heterojunctions are scant. 
Herein, we report the preparation of an S-scheme CdS-In2O3 
heterojunction by the growth of CdS QDs on the surface of 
In
2O3 nanotubes for photocatalytic H2 generation. The distinc-
tive hollow nanotube structure endows the composite photo-
catalyst with a larger specific surface area and abundant H 2 
generation sites, while the formation of S-scheme heterostruc-
tures effectively facilitates the separation and transfer of pho-
togenerated carriers in CdS-In2O3 composite. Consequently, the 
photocatalytic H2 production property of the CdS-In2O3 compo-
site is markedly enhanced in comparison to that of the pure 
CdS. Notably, the photocatalytic H
2 production rate of the 
CdS-7%In2O3 composite reaches 2258.59 μmol g −1 h −1, ap-
proximately 12.3 times higher than that of bare CdS. Further-
more, the carrier transfer mechanism is investigated through in 
situ X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) and density func-
tional theory (DFT) calculations, which verifies the S-scheme 
heterojunction mechanism of the CdS-In
2O3 composites. The 
serial characterizations demonstrate that the formation of an 
S-scheme heterojunction between the In
2O3 hollow structure 
and CdS QDs can efficiently facilitate the separation and migra-
tion of photogenerated carriers, thus improving the photocata-
lytic performance. It is anticipated that this study will provide a 
reference for the rational construction of effective S-scheme 
heterojunctions, thereby facilitating their application in the 
field of photocatalytic H
2 production. 
2.  Experimental  
2.1.  Synthesis of In 2O3 hollow nanotubes 
The synthesis of In 2O3 nanotubes was achieved via a 
two-step process [38]. Initially, 60 mg of In(NO3)3·xH2O and 60 
mg of terephthalic acid (C8H6O4) were dissolved in 40 mL of N, 
N-dimethylformamide (DMF) and agitated until complete dis-
solution was achieved. Subsequently, the resulting solution was 
placed in an oil bath at 393 K for 30 min to allow completion of 
the reaction. After cooling the solution to room temperature, a 
hexagonal In-MIL-68 prism was obtained via centrifugation, 
washing, and drying. In the second step, the resulting 
In-MIL-68 prism was annealed in air at 393 K at the heating 
rate of 5 K min
−1 for 2 h. This was followed by further annealing 
at the same heating rate at 773 K for 2 h to obtain In2O3 hollow 
nanotubes. 
2.2.  Preparation of CdS QDs 
First, 1 mmol of CdCl3 and 1.7 mmol of 3-mercaptopropionic 
acid (MPA) were mixed with 20 mL of deionized water, after 
which the pH of the mixed solution was regulated by NaOH 
solution to approximately 10. Subsequently, the solution was 
transferred to a three-necked flask, which was purged with Ar 
to exhaust air. A solution of 5 mL Na
2S (0.2 mol L −1) was in-
jected into the aforementioned solution and stirred. The mix-
ture was then heated and stirred at 373 K for 30 min. After the 
solution was cooled, 50 mL of C
2H5OH was added to induce 
precipitation. The product was collected by centrifugation and 
washed with anhydrous ethanol to obtain CdS QDs. 
2.3.  Electrostatic assembly of CdS-In₂O₃ composite 
First, 50 mg of CdS QDs was dissolved in 50 mL of deionized 
water and ultrasonicated for 5 min. Subsequently, a certain 
proportion of positively charged In
2O3 (5% for 2.5 mg, 10% for 
5 mg, 30% for 15 mg) was dissolved in 50 mL of deionized wa-
ter and ultrasonicated for 5 min. Subsequently, the solution 
was added to the aforementioned CdS QDs solution and stirred 
for 3 h to facilitate precipitation and adsorption. Thereafter, the 
product was separated by pumping and filtration, washed with 
50 mL of deionized water, and then dried to collect CdS-In
2O3 
composite.

posite photocatalyst were determined by nitrogen physical

2.5.  Activity evaluation of photocatalytic H
2 evolution 
Typically, 5 mg of the catalyst was added to a quartz reactor, 
followed by the addition of 5 mL of deionized water and 1 mL 
of triethanolamine; thereafter, ultrasonic treatment was per-
formed. Other sacrificial agents in the test merely replaced 
triethanolamine, whereas all other experimental conditions 
remained unaltered. Before the photocatalytic hydrogen pro-
duction test, Ar was injected for 30 min to purge the air from 
the quartz reactor. A 300 W Xe lamp (PLS-SXE300D, Perfect 
Light) with an ultraviolet cut-off filter ( λ ≥ 420 nm) was em-
ployed as the light source for 2 h. Subsequently, a gas chro-
matograph (GC7900, Tian Mei) and thermal conductivity de-
tector (TCD) were employed to sample the reactor using an 
airtight syringe. One milliliter of gas was extracted and injected 
into the gas chromatograph to detect the peak area of the gas 
phase and obtain the H
2 yield after the reaction. Furthermore, 
to assess the stability of the CdS-In 2O3 composite catalyst, five 
cycles were conducted under identical conditions. 
3.  Results and discussion 
The hollow CdS-In2O3 nanotube heterostructures were pre-
pared according to the process depicted in Fig. 1(a). Initially, 
the prepared In 2O3 nanotubes were modified with APTES to 
render them positively charged. Subsequently, APTES-modified 
In2O3 nanotubes and negatively charged CdS QDs were assem-
bled through electrostatic self-assembly, thereby achieving the 
synthesis of the CdS-In
2O3 composite. The Zeta potentials of the 
APTES-modified In2O3 and CdS QDs suspensions dispersed in 
deionized water were tested to verify the feasibility of the 
composite assembly. As demonstrated in Fig. S1 and Fig. 2(b), 
the Zeta potentials of CdS QDs and APTES-modified In
2O3 are 
−30.8 and 17.8 mV, respectively. This confirms that the CdS 
QDs are negatively charged while the APTES-modified In
2O3 is 
positively charged. These results establish important condi-
tions for the successful synthesis of CdS-In 2O3 composite. Fig. 
S2 presents the sample diagrams of In 2O3, CdS QDs and the 
CdS-In2O3 composite. In2O3 appears as a white powder, where-
as pure CdS QDs are dark yellow. Furthermore, the CdS-In 2O3 
composite exhibits a lighter color than the pure CdS QDs and 
appears bright yellow with increasing In 2O3 loading. The 
structure and morphology of the prepared samples are identi-
fied through SEM and TEM. The TEM image in Fig. 1(b) shows 
that the average particle size of the CdS QDs is approximately 8 
nm. Furthermore, as depicted in Fig. 1(c), the lattice spacings of 
CdS QDs are measured to be 0.325 and 0.35 nm, which are con-
sistent with the cubic lattice (002) and (111) reflections of CdS 
[39,40]. Additionally, the SEM image in Fig. 1(d) reveals that 
the obtained In
2O3 has a hollow, nanotube-like structure with a 
rough surface, which is conducive to catalyst growth. As shown 
in Fig. 1(e), CdS QDs are uniformly deposited on the In 2O3 
nanotubes, indicating the successful assembly of the CdS-In2O3 
composite. Simultaneously, Fig. S3 presents TEM images of the 
CdS-7%In
2O3 composites. The size of the CdS QDs remains un-
changed after the assembly with the In 2O3 nanotubes. Moreo-
ver, the energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDS) spectra of 
the CdS-7%In
2O3 composite (Fig. 1(f)) verify the presence of In, 
O, Cd, and S. As illustrated in Fig. 1(g), the elemental mapping 
image demonstrates an even distribution of Cd, S, In, and O 
throughout the structure, indicating a uniform distribution of 
CdS QDs on the surface of the In 2O3 nanotubes. These results 
provide evidence for the successful preparation of CdS-In 2O3 
composites.

Based on the characterization and photocatalytic experi-
mental results of this study, the possible charge transfer pro-
cess and the photocatalytic mechanism of CdS-In 2O3 hetero-
structure in photocatalytic H2 production are discussed in Fig. 
5. Initially, owing to the different Fermi levels ( Ef) of CdS and 
In2O3, when CdS comes in contact with In 2O3, electrons spon-
taneously transfer from In 2O3 to CdS until their Ef values are 
equal. Subsequently, the band edges of CdS bend downward as 
a result of electron accumulation, whereas the band edges of 
In
2O3 bend upward at the interface because of electron deple-
tion. In this instance, a built-in electric field is established be-
tween CdS and In
2O3, in line with the XPS and DFT calculation 
results for the CdS-In2O3 heterojunction. The binding energies 
of In and O increase owing to electron depletion, whereas those 
of Cd and S decrease owing to electron accumulation. Addition-
ally, under visible light exposure, electrons (e
−) absorb energy 
and transition from the VB to the CB in the semiconductor, 
generating electron-hole pairs. The built-in electric field then 
drives the photogenerated electrons from the CB of CdS to the 
VB of In
2O3 through an S-scheme transport mechanism, pro-
moting effective photogenerated carrier separation. Further-
more, the accumulated photogenerated electrons in the CB of 
In2O3 rapidly migrate to its surface and reduce H + to H 2, while 
the strongly oxidizing photoinduced holes in the VB of CdS oxi-
dize TEOA to TEOA +. In summary, the successful construction 
of the S-scheme CdS-In2O3 heterojunction effectively promotes 
photogenerated carrier separation, thus enhancing the photo-
catalytic hydrogen evolution performance. 
4.  Conclusions 
In this study, a simple electrostatic self-assembly method 
was employed to successfully synthesize CdS-In
2O3 composite 
photocatalysts. The CdS-In2O3 heterojunction was established 
by uniformly dispersing CdS QDs on hollow In2O3 nanotubes. In 
comparison to pure CdS QDs, the photocatalytic H 2 generation 
properties and stability of CdS-In 2O3 composites were signifi-
cantly enhanced. When irradiated by visible light, the photo-
catalytic H 2 generation rate of the best CdS-7%In 2O3 photo-
catalyst reached 2258.59 μmol g ‒1 h ‒1, approximately 12.3 
times higher than that of pure CdS QDs. The in situ XPS and DFT 
calculations confirmed the S-scheme heterojunction mecha-
nism of CdS-In
2O3 composite. A series of characterizations 
demonstrated that the formation of  S-scheme CdS-In2O3 het-
erojunctions effectively inhibited the recombination of photo-
generated carriers and promoted the efficient separation and 
transfer of electrons. Additionally, the hollow nanotube struc-
ture of CdS-In
2O3 provided a large surface area, exposing a mul-
titude of reactive sites for the improvement of photocatalytic 
H2 generation performance. This study provides a reference for 
constructing effective S-scheme heterojunctions for photocata-
lytic H2 generation.

合理构建S型CdS量子点/In2O3空心纳米管异质结以增强光催化产氢 
吴永辉, 鄢雨晴, 邓奕翔, 黄微雅, 杨  凯, 卢康强* 
江西理工大学化学化工学院，功能晶态材料化学江西省重点实验室, 江西赣州341000 
摘要: CdS由于具有合适的带隙和易于调节的表面结构 , 被广泛应用于光催化制氢领域 .  CdS具有比H2/H2O电位更负的导
带(CB), 有利于H2的析出.  此外, CdS量子点(QDs)的量子尺寸效应有助于提高光催化性能 , 带隙的可调性使它们能够吸收
更宽范围的可见光.  然而, CdS表现出严重的光生载流子复合现象, 并且易发生空穴氧化光腐蚀, 这极大地限制了其在光催
化领域的应用.  为了解决这些问题, 已经提出了许多有效的策略 , 包括形貌调控、元素掺杂和异质结构建 .  在这些策略中, 
用另一种合适的半导体构建S型异质结是一种很有前途的方法 .  S型异质结的构建使氧化和还原反应能够在不同的位置发
生, 从而促进光生电荷的空间分离.  因此, 合理构建S型异质结构是提高CdS光催化活性的可行途径.   
本文通过静电自组装法成功地构建了CdS QDs和In2O3空心纳米管之间的S型异质结, 并探究了其在光催化产氢中的应
用.  在该体系中, 独特的空心纳米管结构赋予了复合光催化剂更大的比表面积和丰富的 H2析出位点, 而S型异质结的形成
有效地促进了CdS-In2O3复合材料内光生载流子的分离和转移 .  Zeta电位测试结果表明, CdS QDs带负电, 而3-氨丙基三乙
氧基硅烷修饰的In2O3带正电, 这为CdS-In2O3复合材料的静电自组装提供依据.  此外, 通过紫外-可见漫反射光谱、Tauc曲线
和莫特-肖特基图谱等研究了 CdS和In2O3的能级结构, 发现两种材料的能级位置交错排列 , 有利于形成S型异质结.  通过原
位光照X射线光电子能谱(XPS)和密度泛函理论计算研究了载流子的转移机制 , 验证了CdS-In2O3复合材料的S型异质结机
制.  功函数计算结果表明 , In2O3具有比CdS更高的费米能级 (Ef), 有利于电子从 In2O3转移到CdS, 这与原位XPS分析结果一
致.  因此, 与纯CdS相比, CdS-In2O3复合材料的光催化产氢性能显著提升 .  值得注意的是, CdS-7%In2O3复合材料的光催化
H2生产速率达到2258.59 μmol g −1 h −1, 约为纯CdS的12.3倍.  此外, 循环测试结果表明, 纯CdS的光催化活性在循环实验中迅
速下降, 表明存在严重的光腐蚀现象 .  然而, CdS-In2O3复合材料即使在5次循环后仍保持相对较高的活性 , 表明S型异质结
的构建有效地减缓了催化剂的光腐蚀程度 .  系列表征结果表明, In2O3空心纳米管与CdS量子点之间形成S型异质结可以有
效地促进光生载流子的分离和迁移, 从而提高了复合材料的光催化性能.   
综上所述, 合理构建S型异质结是提高材料光催化性能的有效策略.  本文构建了S型CdS QDs/In 2O3空心纳米管异质结, 
并探究了该量子点基S型异质结在光催化析氢中的应用, 阐明了S型异质结构建在光催化产氢领域的关键作用, 为合理构建
有效的复合光催化剂提供了新的见解.
