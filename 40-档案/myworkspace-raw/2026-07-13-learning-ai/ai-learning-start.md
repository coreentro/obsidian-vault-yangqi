# AI Learning Start

## Core judgment

AI can be learned well through sustained practice, feedback, and progressively harder projects. The first goal is not to master the entire field, but to complete one small, verifiable application.

## First milestone

Within seven days, use an AI tool to solve one real problem and record:

1. What problem was addressed.
2. What instructions were given to the AI.
3. What was wrong or insufficient in the first result.
4. How the result was checked and improved.

## Mastery check

Explain in your own words:

- What AI can help with in the chosen task.
- What still requires human judgment.
- How to verify whether the output is reliable.
