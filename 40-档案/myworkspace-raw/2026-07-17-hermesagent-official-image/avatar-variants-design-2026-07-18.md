# Male Avatar Variants Design

## Goal

Create three distinct square male avatars derived from the visual language of the supplied reference image. The avatars represent personality rather than physical likeness.

## Shared visual system

- Young East Asian man, head-and-shoulders portrait, three-quarter profile.
- Pure black-and-white palette with no gray or color.
- Bold ink shapes, crisp negative space, sharp white hair highlights, and a retro manga/logo aesthetic.
- Square composition suitable for an app icon; face and silhouette remain legible at small sizes.
- Original male character rather than a direct gender swap or close copy of the reference character.
- No words, letters, watermark, photorealism, gradients, cluttered background, or exaggerated aggression.

## Variant 1: Scholar

- Calm, observant expression with restrained intellectual warmth.
- Neat medium-short black hair and a clean high collar.
- Slightly finer facial lines and more open negative space.
- Mood: reflective, historically aware, patient, and analytical.

## Variant 2: Fighter

- Firmer jaw, sharper gaze, and subtly windswept short hair.
- Structured high-collar jacket with stronger angular geometry.
- Bolder black masses and more dynamic diagonal highlights.
- Mood: disciplined resolve and practical courage, without hostility or militaristic symbols.

## Variant 3: Thinker-Actor

- Balanced calm and determination; focused gaze directed beyond the frame.
- Short, slightly tousled black hair.
- Minimal geometric emblem on the collar suggesting contradiction and unity, with no text.
- Visual balance between the Scholar's openness and the Fighter's force.
- Mood: critical reason joined with practical action.

## Deliverables

Three separate PNG images saved in this conversation directory with concise English filenames. Each output will be visually checked for subject, monochrome treatment, composition, originality, and absence of unintended text.
