- [stated] Thesis research established around a 20 wt% CeO₂/MgO composite catalyst (calcined at 650°C)
- [stated] Achieves 86.5% chalcone yield via Claisen-Schmidt condensation in DMF at 160°C
- [stated] CeO₂ is the primary Lewis acid active phase; MgO is a basic modifier
- [stated] Preferred framing: CeO₂ as central focus, MgO's role downplayed; no mention of unperformed characterization (XRD, XPS, TPD, Hammett analysis, in-situ FTIR)
- [stated] Key integrated reference: Zhang Z. et al., Catalysis Science & Technology, 2016 (DOI: 10.1039/C5CY01607J) — CeO₂ catalyzing one-pot benzyl alcohol + acetophenone → chalcone cascade
- [stated] HPLC method for simultaneously detecting acetophenone and chalcone: 75:25 acetonitrile/water at 280 nm (compromise wavelength) on a C18 column, Wufeng instrument
- [stated] Benzaldehyde oxidation to benzoic acid identified as a reagent purity issue
- [stated] SEM characterization: pure MgO shows uniform rounded aggregates; MgCe composite is heterogeneous, high-contrast, with plate-like and rod-like structures
- [stated] Explored DRS (Diffuse Reflectance Spectroscopy) and CeO₂ crystallization temperatures in a catalysis context
- [stated] Earlier catalyst optimization: high CeO₂ loading (81 wt%) failed to improve over pure MgO; 10–20 wt% CeO₂ recommended, 20 wt% confirmed optimal via experimental screening
- [stated] Explored CeO₂ nanorod core / MgO shell (core-shell) synthesis using hydrothermal methods and urea-based homogeneous precipitation
- [stated] Prefers information-dense, practically actionable output over aesthetic presentation for catalyst synthesis work
