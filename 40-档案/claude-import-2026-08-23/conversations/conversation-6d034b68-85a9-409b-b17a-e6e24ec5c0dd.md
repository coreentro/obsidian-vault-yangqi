# 通识AI公众号简介撰写

> [!abstract]- 导出摘要
>
> **Conversation Overview**
>
> The person runs a WeChat public account called "通识AI" (Tongshi AI) and asked Claude to write a profile/bio for it. They provided a reference sentence as a stylistic and thematic model: "投身于探索AGI的本质，不做中庸的事，带着好奇心，用最长期的眼光去回答最大的问题" (dedicated to exploring the nature of AGI, doing nothing mediocre, with curiosity, using the longest-term perspective to answer the biggest questions), with the guiding concept that "AI is our partner."
>
> The person clarified twice during the conversation: first, that the reference sentence was meant as a thematic core, not to be copied verbatim; second, that phrases explicitly stating "以AI是我们的伙伴为核心" (with AI as our partner as the core) should be removed entirely and replaced with something that conveys the same meaning more naturally. Claude iterated accordingly, ultimately landing on concise opening alternatives such as "以AI为伙伴" (with AI as partner), "携手AI" (hand in hand with AI), and "与AI结伴" (journeying with AI), each followed by the original reference sentence largely intact. Claude recommended "以AI为伙伴" as the strongest option, explaining that the awkwardness of the original phrasing came from embedding a full clause into a "以…为核心" structure.
