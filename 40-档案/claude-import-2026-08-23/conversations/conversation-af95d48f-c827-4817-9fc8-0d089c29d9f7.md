## Claude

Hey Yangqi — good question to ask about asking questions. Here's what makes one work well, especially since you're in a research context:

**Give context before the ask**
State what you already know or tried, and what the goal is. "How does XRD work?" gets a generic textbook answer. "I'm seeing peak broadening in my CeO₂ XRD after calcination at 600°C — is that more likely particle size or lattice strain?" gets something you can actually use.

**Be specific about the gap**
Pin down exactly where your understanding breaks down. Vague questions get vague answers back.

**One question at a time (mostly)**
If you have three related things, ask them as a numbered list rather than one run-on paragraph — makes it easier to answer each properly.

**Say what "good" looks like**
Do you want a quick pointer, a deep explanation, a worked example, a citation to a paper? Naming the format saves back-and-forth.

**For technical/research questions specifically**, it often helps to include:
- What you observed (data, result, error)
- What you expected
- What you've already tried or ruled out

Is this for something specific — like a question for your advisor, a forum post, or something you're trying to ask me right now? Happy to help you sharpen an actual question if you've got one in mind.

## Claude

Hey Yangqi — 好问题，关于"如何提问"这件事本身就值得琢磨。

**先给背景，再问问题 / Give context before the ask**
说清楚你已经知道什么、试过什么、目标是什么。"XRD 是怎么工作的？"只会得到教科书式的泛泛回答。而"我的 CeO₂ 样品 600°C 煅烧后 XRD 峰变宽了，这更可能是晶粒尺寸的问题还是晶格应变？"这种问法能得到真正有用的回答。

**明确指出你不懂的地方 / Be specific about the gap**
精确定位理解断层在哪。模糊的问题只会换来模糊的答案。

**一次问一个（尽量）/ One question at a time**
如果有几个相关问题，用编号列出来，而不是揉成一大段——这样每个问题都能被认真回答。

**说明你想要什么样的回答 / Say what "good" looks like**
是想要一个简单的指引、深入的解释、一个具体例子，还是文献引用？说清楚格式能省去很多来回沟通。

**对于技术/科研类问题**，通常包含这几点会更好：
- 你观察到了什么（数据、结果、报错）
- 你原本预期是什么
- 你已经尝试过 / 排除过什么

你是在准备问导师的问题、发帖提问，还是现在想问我一个具体的问题？如果你手头有具体问题，我可以帮你打磨一下怎么问。

Good — let me use a real example from your own research area. / 好，我用你研究方向里的真实例子来讲。

**❌ Bad question / 差的提问：**
"CeO₂ catalysts don't work well, why?"
"CeO₂ 催化剂效果不好，为什么？"

Too vague — no data, no context, nobody can answer this. / 太模糊了，没有数据、没有背景，谁也答不出来。

---

**✅ Good question / 好的提问：**

"I'm running the tandem oxidation-condensation route from benzyl alcohol + acetophenone to chalcone over a CeO₂ catalyst. / 我正在用 CeO₂ 催化剂做苯甲醇+苯乙酮串联氧化-缩合合成查尔酮的反应。

Conversion is fine (~80%), but selectivity to chalcone is low — a lot of benzaldehyde is left unreacted. / 转化率还可以（约80%），但对查尔酮的选择性偏低——有大量苯甲醛没有继续反应。

I suspect the oxidation step is faster than the condensation step, so aldehyde builds up. / 我怀疑氧化步骤比缩合步骤快，所以苯甲醛积累了。

Is there a known way to balance the two rates — acid/base site ratio, temperature, or catalyst doping? / 有没有已知的方法能平衡这两个反应速率——比如调整酸碱位点比例、温度，或者掺杂改性？"

---

**Why this works / 为什么这样问有效：**

1. States the system clearly / 明确说明了反应体系
2. Gives real data (conversion %) / 给出了真实数据（转化率）
3. States a hypothesis, not just "it's broken" / 提出了一个假设，而不只是说"不行"
4. Asks a specific, answerable question at the end / 结尾问了一个具体、可回答的问题

That's the shape to copy — for your advisor, a forum, or me. / 这个结构可以套用——无论是问导师、发论坛，还是问我。
